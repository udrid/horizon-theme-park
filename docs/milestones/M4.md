## 1. Goal, in plain words

Two trains can run on one coaster safely, like a real ride: the track is split into **block sections**, and a train
waits until the section ahead is empty. A **ride control panel** by the station (a custom screen, D72, D77) lets you send the train out yourself, see the ride's stats, change its settings and rename it.
A rider who jumps off mid-ride is **flung off** with the car's real speed and direction (D22), if you approve the
private call it needs (C4).

## 3. How block sections work (recommended answers)

```
 station ──▶ lift ──▶ [section A: drop, hills] ──▶ block brake ──▶ [section B: turns] ──▶ brake run ──▶ station
   end of a section      end of a section                end of a section
```

- A **section** runs from one section end to the next. Section ends: the station, the top of the lift, and every
  **block brake** node you place (a 5th wand mode, C1).
- **Rule:** a train may enter a section only when no other train is in it. Otherwise it waits at the end of its
  own section: held in the station, held at the bottom of the lift, or stopped on the block brake (ARCHITECTURE §4).
- A ride with *n* trains needs at least *n* + 1 sections, so one is always free (C2).
- A held train restarts by itself when the section ahead clears, speeding up gently (D58, element acceleration).

## 5. Tests (park-core, no game)

1. Sections: the demo layout splits into the expected sections; a train spanning two sections occupies both.
2. Each train's sections are known at every tick; a train's back car still counts until it leaves.
3. BlockBrake: with the next section taken, the train stops on the brake and holds; when it clears, it leaves.
4. Holds at the station and the lift bottom work the same way.
5. **Two trains on a closed ride never share a section over 10,000 simulated ticks** (MILESTONES).
6. Two trains: never more than 400 commands a tick; seats of both trains move every tick.
7. `create` / `set trains 2` refuse a ride with too few sections, with a clear message.
8. Panel: a Dispatch click is queued and sends the waiting train on the next tick (MILESTONES); Manual mode never
   dispatches by itself; a click from a player whose panel isn't linked does nothing.
9. The panel's link survives save and load.
10. Fling-off: a rider off mid-ride gets exactly one `launch_player` with their seat's velocity (D22) in blocks per
    tick; a rider off in the station gets none (MILESTONES).
11. With `launch_player` "not available", getting off is a plain dismount, as now.

## 6. Choices for you

**C1. Where sections end.**
- **A. Automatically at the station and the lift top, plus "block brake" nodes you place (a 5th wand mode).** Like
  real coasters, where the lift and station are always blocks. **Recommended.**
- B. Only where you place block brakes.

**C2. How many trains.**
- **A. 1 or 2 per ride (`/coaster set <name> trains <1|2>`); rides need at least trains + 1 sections.** **Recommended.**
- B. Any number up to the section limit. More to test, and more entities (D27 budget).

**C3. The dispatch panel.**
- **A. A chest-style menu:** right-click the panel block, and a small menu opens with item "buttons" (Dispatch,
  Auto/Manual) and the status in its title ("Train 1 waiting, 3 riders"). Stable hooks only, quick to build.
  **Recommended.**
- B. A custom screen with real buttons and live text. Nicer, but about three times the work (F4).
- C. No panel: a `/coaster dispatch <name>` command.

**C4. Fling-off (D22, D28): the project's first private Horizon code.**
- **A. Approve one small private call in `host_mixin.rs`** (about 10 lines, pinned to `b5b451de`), with an off switch
  per ride. Also draft a Horizon request for a stable "give a player velocity" hook, for you to post. **Recommended**,
  because getting off should feel like real physics (D22).
- B. Not yet: getting off stays a plain dismount; only the Horizon request.
- C. Never: drop the fling-off.

### Small choices (I'll use these unless you say otherwise)

- **S1.** Block-brake numbers: hold when the next section is taken; otherwise trim to 3 blocks/s (ARCHITECTURE §4).
- **S2.** Panel: Auto mode by default (D56's countdown). Manual: the train waits for Dispatch once someone sits.
  Panel item from `/coaster panel`.
- **S3.** A second train starts held in the station behind the first and leaves only when the section ahead is free.
- **S4.** The fling uses the seat's velocity (D22) divided by 20 (blocks per tick), capped at the game's limit of
  about 3.9 blocks per tick.

## 8. Risks

- **Deadlock:** two trains waiting for each other. Test 5 (10,000 ticks) and the trains + 1 rule guard against it.
- **Private code (C4):** pinned to one Horizon commit, so a Horizon update can't break it until we move the pin;
  moving the pin means re-checking it. Whether your own game window applies the velocity is untested (F5).
- **The panel's menu opening from the right-click hook** (F3) is unconfirmed; the fallback (open it on the next tick)
  is planned.
- **Selector cost** (D25): two trains double the seat moves. Fine for one or two rides.
