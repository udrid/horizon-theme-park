## 1. Goal, in plain words

The train stops being pulled at a fixed speed. A chain lifts it up a hill, gravity takes over, and it races
through a drop and hills, slows on a brake run and stops in the station. You see your speed in km/h while riding.
`park-sim` prints the same ride as a table of numbers, so we can check a layout before anyone rides it.

## 2. What already exists and what M2 adds

- **From M1:** the spline, arc-length table and frames; rails, the car and minecart seats; right-click boarding
  (D48–D50); the order queue and the small Host trait (D43); a train that waits at the start while empty.
- **M2 adds:** `coaster/physics.rs` (one substep, ARCHITECTURE §4), `coaster/elements.rs` (Station, Lift,
  Brake), `coaster/forces.rs` (g-forces, needed for friction and for `park-sim`), a hard-coded hill layout,
  the action-bar speed, and `park-sim`'s CSV output.

## 3. Points to be careful about

1. **Cars must still be aimed *k* ticks ahead (D32, D44)**, but speed now changes. Physics is deterministic (D12),
   so the runtime can run a copy of the train *k* ticks forward to find where the car will be. *k* is still fixed per
   car when it's summoned (`teleport_duration` can't change, D24): work it out from the worst `v² × curvature`
   found by one dry lap at build time (small choice S3).
2. **Friction uses the normal push** (D14), which comes from the g-force maths (D15), so `forces.rs` is needed now,
   not at M5. The HUD g-meter stays out of scope (M5).
3. **No banking in M2** (D24 bakes roll into rails; MILESTONES puts banking in M5). Flat turns at speed push riders
   sideways, so the layout must keep lateral g low. The layout is checked by `park-sim` tests, not by eye (S1).
4. **Riders get off normally** (no fling-off yet, D28). A rider who gets off mid-ride is just left where they are.
5. **Seat moves stay by tag** (D49): every seat every tick costs about 1 µs per entity in the world. The hill layout
   has about 120 rail pieces, so the world holds roughly 130 entities: about 0.8 ms a tick for 6 seats. Fine for M2.

## 5. Tests (park-core, no game)

From ARCHITECTURE §4 (D12–D15):
1. Frictionless closed loop: speed within 1% of the start after 100 laps.
2. Same ride run twice: identical `s` and `v` at every tick.
3. Frictionless 40-block drop from rest: bottom speed 28.01 blocks/s ± 0.5%.
4. Flat unbanked circle, radius 10, at 10 blocks/s: lateral 1.02 g, vertical 1.00 g, ± 1%.
5. Flat straight at 20 blocks/s with default friction and drag: 16.46 ± 0.1 blocks/s after 100 blocks.
6. A 6-car train cresting a hill: the back car's speed at the top is greater than the front car's was.
7. Frames: no flip through an S-bend; closed loop `up` matches within 0.1° (already from M1).

Elements and layout:
8. Lift: the train never rolls back on the lift, and leaves it at the chain speed.
9. Brake: the train leaves the brake run at `target_v` ± 0.1.
10. Station: the train stops within 0.2 blocks of the stop point and holds for the dwell time.
11. Demo layout, one simulated lap: the train never stalls (except in the station), and finishes the lap.
12. Demo layout limits (D58): drops 50–65°; vertical g at most 4.0 in valleys; airtime hills between 0 and −0.5 g; lateral g at most 1.0; vertical g changes by at most 3 g over any 1-second window; top speed at most 30 blocks/s.

Runtime with `FakeHost`:
13. On a car tick, the car's `tp` goes to the position the simulation says it will reach *k* ticks later.
14. Riders get an action-bar line with their speed in km/h (C5's rhythm); nobody else does.
15. Station flow (C3): no dispatch while empty; the countdown starts when someone sits.
16. Several cars: one summon per car and per seat; every seat moves every tick; never more than 400 commands a tick.

## 6. Choices for you

**C1. What `/coaster demo` builds.**
- **A. The new hill layout replaces the oval in the game; the oval stays only as a test track.** One demo, the
  interesting one. **Recommended.**
- B. Both: `/coaster demo oval` and `/coaster demo hills`. A second command to keep working.

**C2. How many cars.**
- **A. A 3-car train, 2 seats per car (6 seats).** The physics treats the train as one body anyway (D13), and it gives
  MILESTONES' "front row vs back row" feel. About 9 entities more. **Recommended.** (Brings part of M4's
  "multi-car trains" forward.)
- B. One car until M4, as MILESTONES has it. Less to build now; no back-row difference to feel.

**C3. How the station works.**
- **A. Automatic:** when someone sits, a 5-second countdown shows on the action bar, then the train leaves. Every lap
  ends with a stop in the station: riders can get off, or stay and go again after the next countdown. If nobody is
  aboard, the train waits. **Recommended.**
- B. Like M1: it runs non-stop while anyone is aboard, and stops only when everyone is off.
- C. A `/coaster go` command sends the train out.

**C4. Element numbers** (ARCHITECTURE §4 marked them OPEN for M2).
- **A. Lift chain 5 blocks/s (18 km/h); brakes slow by 6 blocks/s² down to 3 blocks/s; the station pushes out at
  2 blocks/s; dwell 5 seconds (C3).** Close to real coasters, quick enough to test. **Recommended.**
- B. The original proposals: lift 4 blocks/s and a 10-second dwell. Slower lift, longer wait.

**C5. Speed readout.**
- **A. The action bar shows the speed in km/h, updated every 5 ticks (4 times a second).** Readable, not flickery.
  **Recommended.**
- B. Updated every tick. It flickers at the last digit.

## 8. Risks

- **Flat turns at speed** may feel harsh until banking (M5). Test 12 caps lateral g at 1.5.
- **Steep drops:** cars use `tp` pitch only (no roll, D24). A very steep drop near 90° may make the car's yaw
  jump. The layout keeps drops under about 60°.
- **Seats falling between moves** (D50 note) may show as jitter at high speed. If it does, the builder's noted fix
  (resetting the minecart's motion each tick) is tried first.
- **Selector cost** grows with the world's entities (D25). Fine at about 130 entities; a packed park needs the
  Horizon UUID fix (#4966) or private code later.
