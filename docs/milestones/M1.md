## 1. Goal, in plain words

Type `/coaster demo` and a small oval track (about 60 blocks) appears in front of you: rails drawn with display
entities and one car going round at a steady speed. Type `/coaster ride` and you sit in the car for a full lap.
No gravity yet (M2), no building tools or saving (M3), one car only (M4).

## 3. How a tick works in M1

```
 /coaster demo|ride|remove ─▶ native_perform_command ─▶ glue: queue the order, ran = true, "OK" feedback
 native_server_tick(End) ─▶ glue ─▶ Runtime::tick(&mut host):
     1. apply queued orders (build the demo, seat a rider, remove)
     2. move the train:  s = s + v·0.05   (constant speed, no physics yet)
     3. car: every k ticks, one `tp` to where it will be k ticks later, with yaw and pitch    [D24, D32]
     4. seats (every tick, ridden or not): `tp @e[tag=htp_seat_demo_<i>,limit=1] X Y Z yaw pitch`   [D49]
        rider on = a player within 0.5 block of seat + 0.71875 up; off = no player within 2 blocks
        the train waits at s = 0 while empty, runs at 8 blocks/s while anyone rides, and when the
        last rider is off it finishes the lap and stops at s = 0                              [D49]
     5. greeting (M0's Welcome)
```

## 4. The new Host trait (D38): a small "game door"

```rust
// host.rs: everything park-core may ask of the game. Nothing else.
pub struct PlayerInfo { pub uuid: u128, pub name: String, pub pos: DVec3, pub dimension: String }

pub trait Host {
    fn players(&self) -> Vec<PlayerInfo>;      // placed players only
    fn run(&mut self, command: &str) -> bool;  // a console command; true = it ran without failing
    fn broadcast(&mut self, text: &str);       // chat to everyone + log
    fn tell(&mut self, player: u128, text: &str);
    // Later, private fallbacks are added here as extra methods with a default that says
    // "not available" (for example launch_player for D28), so one trait stays the only door [D7].
}
```

- `commands.rs` holds **every command's exact text** as small pure functions (`summon_rail(…) -> String`,
  `move_car(…)`, `move_seat(…)`). The seat's kind (minecart, or a saddled invisible pig) lives in one place there [D49]. `runtime.rs` decides *what* to send; the glue just runs the strings.
- `FakeHost` records every call in order and lets a test say "this command fails". Runtime tests assert the exact
  list of strings.
- At most 400 commands per tick [D21]; the rest wait, with one log warning.

## 6. Tests (park-core, no game)

1. Frames: no flip through an S-bend; on a closed loop `up` comes back within 0.1°.
2. Same track twice: identical table and frames (deterministic).
3. The demo oval's table length is within 0.1% of its exact length (2·11 + 2π·6 = 59.70 blocks); use enough nodes.
4. Table lookup at s and at s + length gives the same point (closed tracks wrap).
5. Number text: 4 decimals, `.` decimal point, no exponent, `-0` written as `0.0000`.
6. Exact text of every command in `commands.rs` (summon rail with its quaternion and scale, car `tp` with yaw/pitch,
   seat move by name, summon seat pig, mount, kill by tag).
7. Rail pieces: straights use 4-block pieces, curves shorter; every piece's sag ≤ the limit (S3); pieces cover the
   whole track with no gap or overlap.
8. Yaw/pitch: forward +Z → yaw 0, +X → yaw −90, −Z → 180, climbing 45° → pitch −45 (Minecraft's convention).
9. Runtime with `FakeHost`: `demo` gives the rail summons, then the car summon, then the two seat summons; never more than 400 commands a tick.
10. Every tick one `tp` per seat by its tag (2 per tick), ridden or not; on car ticks the car `tp` comes first, aimed *k* ticks ahead.
11. The train holds at s = 0 while empty, starts when a player is within 0.5 block of a rider point, and when the rider is off (nobody within 2 blocks) finishes the lap and stops at s = 0.
12. `ride` is only a hint ("right-click a seat in the car"), with no commands; `remove` → `kill @e[tag=htp_demo]`.

## 7. Choices for you

**C1. Curve type** (ARCHITECTURE marks it OPEN for M1).
- **A. Centripetal Catmull-Rom: the track passes exactly through every node, never overshoots or loops.** Easy to
  build with in Minecraft later (place a node, the track goes through it). **Recommended.**
- B. Bézier with handles you drag (like NoLimits 2). More control, but much harder to edit in a block world.

**C2. The Host trait (D38).**
- **A. A small "game door" (section 4): players, run a command, chat.** All command text lives in park-core and
  is tested word for word; the glue stays about 50 lines. Private fallbacks are added later as extra methods.
  **Recommended.**
- B. The original "ride-level" trait (`spawn_display`, `move_display`, `seat`, …) with a translator into commands.
  One more layer to write and test, for the same commands.

**C3. How often the car is moved (*k*, D32).** Because `teleport_duration` can't change after summon (F7), *k* is
fixed for each car when it appears.
- **A. Worked out once per ride from its tightest curve and speed:** the largest *k* from 1 to 5 whose straight
  glide strays at most 0.05 block from the track. Nothing to set by hand. **Recommended.**
- B. A per-coaster setting, default 5. Simple, but on a tight curve the car visibly cuts the corner.
- C. Always 1 (every tick). Smoothest, 5 times the car commands.

**C4. Seats.**
- **A. A seat exists only while someone rides it:** `/coaster ride` summons an invisible **pig** at the car's
  seat, mounts you, and the pig is removed when you get off. No empty seat can wander off (F2), and no chicken eggs
  on the track. **Recommended.** (Amends D31's "empty seats moved by tag".)
- B. Seats always exist and travel with the car (D31 as written): empty ones move by the slow tag selector, and
  since Horizon ignores `NoAI`, they may wander between moves.

**C5. Commands.**
- **A. `/coaster demo` builds the oval 3 blocks in front of you (replacing an old one), `/coaster ride` seats you,
  `/coaster remove` removes it. Everyone can use them.** **Recommended.**
- B. The same, but operators only.
- C. The oval appears by itself at a fixed spot when a world opens.

**C6. The content mod.**
- **A. Mod id `htp`, so items are `htp:rail` and `htp:car` with no extra settings; simple cube models using vanilla
  textures (no drawing needed).** You can redraw them in Blockbench (a free model editor) any time later.
  **Recommended.**
- B. Mod id `horizon-theme-park-content` with `namespaces = ["htp"]`. Longer, same items.

### Small choices (I'll use these unless you say otherwise)

- **S1.** The oval: two 11-block straights and two half circles of radius 6 (59.7 blocks), flat, 3 blocks above
  your feet. Speed 8 blocks/s (about 29 km/h), so a lap takes about 7.5 s. Ride id `demo`.
- **S2.** Getting off mid-ride in M1 is a plain dismount (you stay where you are). The fling-off (D22, D28) needs
  the private call and comes later.
- **S3.** Rail pieces: 4, 3, 2 or 1 block long, the longest whose sag is at most 0.03 block (D29).
- **S4.** Tags: every entity `htp` and `htp_demo`; the car also `htp_car_demo_0`, the seat `htp_seat_demo_0`. No
  chosen UUIDs in M1 (`tp <uuid>` can't find displays anyway).
- **S5.** Seat and car heights are constants tuned in game during T8 (a start: car 0.5 block above the rails).

## 15. First in-game test (owner, 2026-10-31) and the fix

First build (7 min 50 s) loaded; `/coaster demo` drew the rails, the red car and two minecarts; the train ran smoothly. Two problems: the train did not start when the owner sat down (it ran a lap only after they got off), and the minecarts looked sunk inside the red car.
Cause, from the owner's reading while seated: rider y 79.1336, seat y 80.3461, same x/z: the rider hangs **1.2125 below** the seat, not 0.71875 above it (that was the mob number from spike R7). With a 0.5 "on" limit the mod saw a rider 1.93 away: not close enough to count, not far enough to count as off.
Fix: `RIDER_LIFT` = -1.2125 and `SEAT_LIFT` 0.1 → 1.5 (rider's feet just above the car floor, head above the shell). Probable reason for the extra -0.8: minecarts fall at top speed between our moves because only their position is reset, not their speed; if the ride looks jittery, add a per-tick `Motion` reset (a scan per seat). Needs one more build to test.
